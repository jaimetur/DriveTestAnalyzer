How it works

One flow, four steps

Everything a drive test campaign needs, from the raw files to the slides in front of the customer.

Load

Upload the CDRs, logs and network inventories of each campaign. They are processed and ready in minutes.

Score

Score every operator with your methodology and see who wins, by KPI, Region, city and campaign.

Explain

Find where the points are lost, what the radio says and the root cause of every failed call.

Deliver

Generate the PowerPoint and Word reports, on demand or on a schedule, with live charts and comments.
