                        Custom rules

                          How the strategies work

                          The Strategy gives every call its step, its queue and its Priority. Dual Queue (Cities first) and Dual Queue (Roads first) give every call a step of two lists (list 1 during the campaign, list 2 once its Final CDR is scored) and put it in one of two queues, Voice and Data, whose analysts take the top call; Custom rules use the rules of the workspace, tried from top to bottom (the first one whose conditions a call meets decides: Prioritized when the call meets its criterion, else Track only).

                          Every field of a step can change. By default, each step has its own Priority, from P1 to P8, and takes the Focused Operators (the Focus operators of the Operator Maps). The steps of one Priority are grouped under it and tried in their order: drag a step by its handle (or move it with the arrow keys) to change its place or Priority. A call meets a step by its conditions, each joined to the one before by AND or OR, AND first (Every call and Focused Operators stand alone). A call of an SA campaign takes its SA step when the step takes its operator, else its NSA step (an operator without SA, such as Three); a call no step takes is tracked when a Tracked step takes it. Inside a step, the calls with the highest Expected Gain go first (the points their failure lost in the Final CDR of the latest campaign, or their own once the Final CDR of their campaign is scored), then the oldest. Each queue takes its calls by Priority, the newest campaign first.
