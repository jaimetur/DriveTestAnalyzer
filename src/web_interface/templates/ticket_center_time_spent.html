How it is measured
  • The time of a ticket is the calendar time from the moment it was opened to the moment it was resolved or closed, 24 hours a day (nights and weekends included): the tool does not record working hours.
  • Only the tickets resolved or closed in the period chosen count (this week from Monday, this month, quarter or year, or all of them); the open and rejected ones are left out. The totals add up the time of those tickets, and the average and median are per ticket.
  • Where the time goes splits that time by the status the ticket had, from the changes of status of its timeline (a ticket starts New).
  • Units: d days of 24 hours, h hours, m minutes (13d 4h, 7h 12m).
