Campaign Maps

Collapse
{% if active_workspace %}

How every chart, table, legend, filter and PowerPoint or Word report shows and orders the campaigns of "{{ active_workspace.name }}". The label format applies to every campaign, so new campaigns follow it without editing the map; filters and stored CDR values keep the original campaigns.

Label format

Markers: {year} 2026, {yy} 26, {quarter} 2, {mode} SA or NSA, {market} the country code such as UK. Characters inside the braces of a marker are written only when the campaign has that part: {-mode} writes -SA for an SA campaign and nothing for the others. Campaigns without a year and quarter keep their name.

Order inside a quarter

Campaigns are ordered by year and quarter, and the campaigns of one quarter in this order.

    Exceptions

    Campaigns that do not follow the pattern get their own label. They are ordered by the year and quarter of their label (after the campaigns of that quarter), or first, in this order, when the label has none. No exceptions are needed while every campaign follows the format.
