Compatibility of P3 CDRs with DriveTest Analyzer

The comparison of P3 CDRs with the NetCheck CDRs the tool is built for, as the Umlaut CDR Compatibility report does for Umlaut: which fields each module uses, what each would have with P3, and how to adapt the tool without losing features.

Pending a P3 sample · 24 October 2026Branch 0.9.0NetCheck reference: UK Q3 2026 SA and NSA CDRs

Not analysed yet: there is no sample of P3 CDRs, so their files, fields and values cannot be compared with NetCheck yet.

The base is ready. P3 CDR is listed as a coming-soon CDR type, and every module reads the CDR columns through the field profile of the workspace’s CDR type (phase 1, done in version 0.9.0). P3 needs its reader, its profile and its content, with the same model as Umlaut.

Same model: one provider per workspace, with the P3 names and values visible to its users; its own Scoring methodology, PPT templates and NQ rules; the DT Analyzer RCA, available in every workspace, as the common engine.

What is known

  • The Umlaut files of the sample link to a P3 path (p3path:\\aws-fs.p3-aws.com), so the P3 format may be similar to the Umlaut one.
  • Like every provider, P3 will be read with its native names: nothing is translated into NetCheck names.

What the analysis will cover

The same sections as the Umlaut CDR Compatibility report, as soon as a sample is available.

  • Material analysed: the files of the sample, their CDR type, rows and columns.
  • Format differences: organisation of the files, granularity, call identity, campaign, environment, statuses, units, sides of the call, radio, failures and names.
  • Fields per CDR type: what each provider has that the other does not, and every NetCheck field the tool uses with its P3 equivalent: Direct, Conversion, Derivable, Approximate or Missing.
  • Compatibility per module: what ingestion, Builders, CDR Analysis, Reporting, Scoring & GAP Analysis, PPT Designer, Non-Qualified Calls, Network Insights and the Workspace Config mappings need with P3.
  • Scoring KPIs: which KPIs have equivalent data in P3, until P3 has its own methodology.
  • Limits of 100%: the data P3 does not bring, the definitions that change and what P3 adds.

What is needed

  1. A sample of P3 CDRs (Data, Voice and Speech), ideally multi-operator and, if possible, with NR SA and walk or train tests.
  2. The P3 Scoring methodology (KPIs, thresholds, weights and categories), if P3 workspaces will be scored with it.
  3. Whether P3 delivers any RCA or failure classification besides the CDRs.
