# Reporting

Use **Reporting** to build a **Bundle Report Template**, one PowerPoint or Word document with the parts you choose of PPT Dashboards, Scoring & GAP Analysis, Network Insights, Non-Qualified Calls and CDR Analysis, and to collect artifacts from several modules in one **Reporting Job**, run it on demand or on a schedule, and email the artifacts with a clear description of each one and its filters.

> [!NOTE]
> **Module access.** **Reporting** is in every Modules Profile of a new deployment. Super-admins choose who sees it in **Super Admin → Modules Profiles**. Creating, editing and running jobs, and saving or deleting favourite Bundle Report Templates, requires `user-editor`, `admin` or `super-admin`. A Bundle Report Template only includes the sections of the modules activated for the account that generates it.

## Presentation

The presentation **Reporting** sums the module up. One PowerPoint or Word document with the parts you choose of every module, and Reporting Jobs that build and email them on a schedule. Select it to open it in the slide viewer, play it full screen or download its PowerPoint; every presentation is also in [Product Presentations](presentations.md).

## Bundle Report Templates

The **Bundle Report Templates** panel, above the Reporting Jobs, builds one document from five sections, by default in this order: **Scoring & GAP Analysis**, **Network Insights**, **Non-Qualified Calls**, **PPT Dashboards** and **CDR Analysis** (each with the title its module has in Interface Settings; a saved favourite keeps its own order, and favourites saved before CDR Analysis was a section have it unchecked).

| Part | What it chooses |
| --- | --- |
| **Common filters** | The **NR Mode**, on its own row, then Operators, Operator_Vendor, Vendor_Operator, Vendors, Regions, Clusters and Cities (with **Main Cities**, the Main Cities of the workspace at each export), as in Scoring & GAP Analysis. They apply to every module and replace the saved filters of the Dashboards; empty filters include every value |
| **Campaigns** | Each module has its own, at the top of its card and in amber so it stands out (as are the Campaigns of the Scoring Trends): **Latest campaign**, **Latest 2 campaigns**, **Latest 3 campaigns**, **Latest 4 campaigns**, **Previous campaign**, **All available campaigns** (which follow the newest campaigns at each export) or chosen campaigns of the NR Mode, every campaign when none is chosen. They choose the CDRs of the module, so for example Scoring can compare the latest two campaigns while Network Insights shows only the latest one; in PPT Dashboards they replace the Campaign filter of every Dashboard. Network Insights has one **Campaign**: **Latest campaign** (the default), **Previous campaign** or one campaign (a section saved with several keeps the newest one) |
| **Modules** | Each module with its checkbox and its sub-modules: the saved Dashboards of the NR Mode (each Dashboard is made for one NR Mode, shown beside its name), the results tabs of Scoring & GAP Analysis (Summary, Scoring Charts, Scoring Tables, Geographical Areas, Scoring Trends, GAP Analysis and Points Lost), the panels of Network Insights and Non-Qualified Calls and the CDR types of CDR Analysis (CDR Data, CDR Voice and CDR Speech, each with the parts of the analysis of each CDR: CDR Summary, Selected Metric Cards, Visual Analytics of each metric and Processed Metrics). Below its **Campaigns** (and, in Scoring & GAP Analysis, its GAP reference and Methodology), each module shows its **Options** and then its **Sub-modules** (its **Dashboards** in PPT Dashboards), each in a subpanel of its own. **Charts & tables** unfolds the charts and tables of a sub-module to include the whole sub-module or only some of them |
| **Order** | Drag a module or a sub-module by its handle (⋮⋮), or focus the handle and use the arrow keys, to order the modules and the sub-modules inside each module |
| **Scoring & GAP Analysis options** | Best Network and/or Most Reliable Network; All Environments (all of them combined) and/or each environment; beside the Campaigns of the module, the **GAP reference** and the **Methodology** (**By campaign** by default; its recommendations, individual GAP comparisons and Points Lost views focus on the Focus operators of the [Operator Maps](workspace-config.md#operator-maps), and the Operators of the common filters take [patterns](web-interface.md#operator-patterns) such as `VF*`); **Show KPI values** and **Show GAP values** in the scoring tables; **Export All Operators in Focus panels** (every operator in the panels that show the Focus operators; the components list the panels of [Scoring Export Panels](workspace-config.md#scoring-export-panels) that are exportable, those of the Focus operators tagged **Focus**); **Compare NSA vs SA**, offered when the workspace has NSA and SA CDRs (the module scores the NSA and SA CDRs of its campaigns side by side whatever the NR Mode of the report, its Campaigns listing those of both NR Modes, and its GAP reference can then be **Same operator in NSA**; see [NSA vs SA](scoring-gap-analysis.md#nsa-vs-sa)), with, below it, how the document shows each NR Mode, as the results of the page do: **NSA vs SA** (**Each panel: NSA then SA**, the default, or **All NSA, then all SA**, the slides of each NR Mode tagged **NSA** or **SA**) and **NSA & SA charts** (**NSA & SA in the same chart**, the default, where the Scoring Charts show the NSA and SA bars of each operator and the Scoring Trends and National & Geographical Areas Trends an NSA line and a dashed SA line per operator on one axis of quarters, or **NSA & SA in separate charts**); and the **Geographical Areas & Scoring Trends**, always included and scored on the whole country (the Region, Cluster and City filters do not apply to them): in **Geographical Areas**, the **Breakdown** (Region by default, Cluster, City or None) and the **Separate cities** (London by default, or the **Main Cities** of the workspace at each export) of the Geographical Areas and the National & Geographical Areas Trends; in **Scoring Trends**, their **Campaigns** (**All available campaigns** by default, Latest campaign, Latest 2, 3 or 4 campaigns, Previous campaign or campaigns; none, **Same as the module**, takes the campaigns of the module), with which the Scoring Trend and the National & Geographical Areas Trends are calculated, and their **Time split**. An identical completed calculation (a Saved Filter with the same CDRs, filters, methodology and GAP reference, and the same Scoring Trends campaigns when they have some) is reused; otherwise the scoring is calculated |
| **Network Insights options** | Technology, Split by, the LTE and NR coverage and interference thresholds, the overshooting distance, the map grid and the Environments |
| **Non-Qualified Calls options** | The period of the progress timeline, every call or open calls only, whether the Root Cause Analysis counts the suggested root causes, and whether its charts show the Not classified calls and the Lifecycle the calls without a value |
| **CDR Analysis options** | The **Global Comparison** (Auto by default) and **Global CDF Comparison** (Operator by default) of the charts and the metrics of each CDR type (every metric, also those added later, when none is chosen), as the CDR Analysis artifact of a Reporting Job. Its Campaigns choose the CDRs of the NR Mode of the report that the combined CDR tables include (the Final CDR of a campaign before its Weekly and Daily CDRs, or the **In Combined?** choice), the common filters filter their rows, and each CDR starts with a cover named after it, followed by the parts chosen for its type |

**Generate** creates the report in the background, in PowerPoint and in Word with the preview of both, and opens a dialog with its progress and its processing time. **Cancel** stops it; **Hide** (or Escape, or ×) closes the dialog while the report keeps being generated, so you can go on using the application: **Generate** then shows its progress and time and opens the dialog again (also when you come back to Reporting while the report is generated), and when the report is ready a dialog on any page of the application offers to open its preview in Reporting. **Open last report**, beside **Generate**, opens the preview of the last report you generated in the panel whenever there is one, to check it or download it again: the workspace keeps it with its documents (in `output/reports/bundle-reports/`), also after a restart, until you generate another one. Ready, the dialog takes almost the whole screen to preview it before **Download**: **PowerPoint** and **Word**, at its top, choose the format previewed and downloaded (the PowerPoint slide by slide, the Word document page by page, in portrait; each report generated opens in PowerPoint), and switching between them is immediate and keeps the section shown; on the left, every section with its slides or pages (select a section or a page to go to it); on the right, the chosen page, which zooms with the wheel (towards the pointer), by dragging a rectangle over it or with **+**, **−** and **⤢** (the whole page, also with a double click), and moves with Shift+drag or the middle button; **‹ ›** or the arrow keys show the other pages. Sections that cannot be generated (for example Scoring without a campaign with Data, Voice and Speech CDRs) are listed in the dialog and left out; the others stay in the report. A Word page never repeats the title of a component: one that follows on the same page another with the same title and context goes without it. The preview needs LibreOffice; without it the list on the left shows the title of each slide.

Each section generated is kept for the next exports: **Reuse generated sections** (checked by default) uses it again while the application has not been updated and its options, the common filters and the data it reads have not changed (the CDRs, the Operator and Vendor Maps, the Main Cities and, per section, the Dashboards, the methodologies and the Saved Filters calculated, the inventory of the analysis, the follow-up of the calls or the CDRs the Campaigns of CDR Analysis choose): a Saved Filter calculated again generates the Scoring & GAP Analysis section again by itself, in the exports and in the Reporting Jobs alike, so exporting the same report in the other format only composes and renders it, and exporting it again in the same format also uses the document composed (and the Word pages packed) by the earlier export and its preview; the dialog names the sections reused and whether the document was too, and the run of a Reporting Job lists them in the details of its artifact (*Reused from an earlier export*). Uncheck it to generate every section again. Two exports that need the same section at once generate it once. Scoring & GAP Analysis uses the Saved Filter of the same CDRs, filters, methodology and GAP reference when there is one (a Favourites Filter first), with its own Split by and National & Area Summary, and Network Insights the analysis already made for the same selection and inputs, so neither is calculated again.

**Theme**, in violet beside **Title on the cover** and **Subtitle**, is the one colour theme of the whole report: the charts, tables and slide titles of every section that supports themes (Scoring & GAP Analysis, Network Insights, PPT Dashboards and CDR Analysis; Ericsson by default, see [Theme](network-insights.md#theme)). Favourites saved when their Scoring and Network Insights sections kept the theme store only the theme of the report (that of their Scoring section) the first time the application opens their workspace.

The document starts with a cover with a large title (**Title on the cover**, else the name of the favourite), the optional **Subtitle** under it and every common filter (All for those without a value), and each section starts with its own cover: its name, its Campaigns, the common filters and the options chosen for it. Covers and transitions use the Vodafone-Three red of the template (`Template_VodafoneThree.pptx`) with the Ericsson mark drawn in outline behind them and the Vodafone-Three and Ericsson logos at the top right. Every cover has the same title and subtitle sizes (54 and 24 pt) and every transition smaller ones (40 and 20 pt), whatever the module; the subtitle starts right below the room of two title lines, so a long title wraps into it. Each transition shows its counter above its title: 01, 02… for the first level and 2.1, 2.1.1… below it. Each section cover is a transition of the first level (01 Scoring & GAP Analysis, 02 Network Insights…) and the transitions of its module are numbered below it: each scoring (1.1) and environment (1.1.1), each Dashboard (4.1) and the transitions of its PPT Template (4.1.1); in the Word version they head their pages with the same counter. Each chart or table is a component that never spans two slides or two pages:

- **PowerPoint.** A table spread over several slides becomes one table on one slide: its rows joined under one header, and the parts of a table too wide for one slide (each repeating its first column) joined into one table with all its columns. A long table continues in up to four tables side by side, each under the header, so its text stays readable, and its columns are as wide as their values need; when even so its values would be smaller than 6 pt, it continues on two or more slides (its title ending in *· 1/2*, *· 2/2*…), each under the header (in Word, smaller than 6 pt on the page). Tables side by side spread over several slides (such as one per network in the Network Deployment views) are joined each with its continuation and go on together on as few slides as keep them legible, each slide with the same rows of every table (a table that has run out of rows leaves its place empty). Other charts or tables spread over several slides share one slide, made smaller to fit down to 60 % of their size; smaller, they continue on more slides, as few as keep them at that size. Small consecutive components of a section share a slide when they fit almost at their size, each under its own title (its subtitle when its title is the title of the slide).
- **Word.** Portrait pages: the report cover fills a page and is followed by a **Contents** page in the fonts and colours of the report theme (on its dark background for a dark theme): each section with its number, the parts of each section and the topics of their components (such as Where to act or GAP Analysis, the components of every operator together), each with its page and a link to it; a long one continues on more pages. Then the cover of each section fills a page and the components of the section follow it. Each page holds as many components of its section as fit, in their order: a page takes the next components while they fit (made a little smaller when needed), and only reorders them inside the page, for example to put two narrow components side by side, so a component never goes after one that follows it. A part, a topic, a component or a group of its pages that does not fit whole in what is left of a page starts the next one, so a page only takes what fits on it whole; one larger than a page starts a page and continues on the next ones. A component larger than a page fills a page on its own, made smaller. The covers of the scorings and of the Dashboards become headings. A title too long for one line is made smaller, in PowerPoint and in Word, so it never covers the chart or table under it.

The PowerPoint and Word exports of [Scoring & GAP Analysis](scoring-gap-analysis.md), [Network Insights](network-insights.md) and [Non-Qualified Calls](non-qualified-calls.md) (on their page and as Reporting Jobs artifacts) are composed in the same way from the same slides, each starting with the cover of its module and ending with the closing slide, so they always look like their section of a Bundle Report Template.

Save the panel as a **favourite** to use it again: enter its **Name** and select **★ Save as favourite** (a name that a favourite already has overwrites it once you confirm it; another name saves a new favourite). **Favourite** loads a saved report, **Rename** gives it another name (keeping what it saved; every Reporting Job based on it takes the new name, also one renamed in the Job Editor, and the dialog lists them) and **Delete** (for admins, super-admins and the user who created the favourite) removes it after listing the Reporting Jobs that generate it: those based on it are disabled, and every job that generated it says in its Artifacts that the associated Bundle Report Template no longer exists (its runs fail until another favourite is chosen). The panel remembers the last report edited in this browser. Favourites are kept in the workspace (the Bundle Report Templates table of Database Management) and every save, rename, deletion, export and download is recorded in App Events, with the Reporting Jobs renamed or disabled. Generated documents are kept in `output/reports/bundle-reports/` of the workspace for a day.

## Artifacts

A job includes any combination of these artifacts. A user can only include artifacts of the modules activated for their account, and each run checks it again with the permissions of the job's author.

| Artifact | Content | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CDR Analysis** | The CDR Analysis export of every CDR its NR Mode and Campaigns choose: global KPIs, metric cards, CDF and comparison charts, grouped percentiles and the processed metrics table | PowerPoint and/or Word; **NR Mode** and **Campaigns** (which choose the CDRs, see below); in a **Metrics** row, the metrics of each CDR type (CDR Data, Voice and Speech; all by default); in a **Filters** row, the Operator, Operator_Vendor, Vendor, Region, Cluster and City (with Main Cities) filters of Network Insights, with the values of the CDRs of the NR Mode; and two single-choice rows, **Global Comparison** for the charts' grouping and **Global CDF Comparison** for the CDF curves (Operator by default) |
| **Network Insights** | RF quality overview with campaign deltas, the Key Findings slides, RSRP and SINR CDFs, coverage and interference maps with their weakest areas (LTE and NR separately for LTE+NR), observed and licensed spectrum (with the NR sites by duplex mode, the bandwidth per band and the primary bandwidth of the download tests) and network deployment | One report per entry, in PowerPoint and/or Word: NR Mode, technology, LTE/NR thresholds, grouping (Operator, Vendor, Region, Cluster, City, Environment, CDR type, Campaign; Operator stays checked unless Vendor is checked, as in the module), **Campaigns** (which choose the CDRs, see below), the **Theme** of its charts and tables and the Operator/Operator_Vendor/Vendor/Region/Cluster/City filters in one row |
| **Dashboard** | One new Dashboard PowerPoint per entry, generated at each run | NR Mode first, which lists its Dashboards, then the Dashboard's own options, prefilled with its saved definition: Scope and Vendor comparison, date range and every Adaptative Filter, the **Campaigns** first (they choose the CDRs, see below), then Operator, Operator_Vendor, Vendor, Market, Region, Cluster, City (with **Main Cities** first: the Main Cities of the workspace at each run), RAT, Session Type, Call Status and the Dashboard's Auto-calculated Fields. Filter values are those of the CDRs of the NR Mode that the other filters leave; empty filters include every value. **Auto comments** writes [generated comments](ppt-dashboards.md#generated-comments) into the PPT (Off by default; on the slides without comments, in place of the generated ones or in place of every comment) with its **Comment writer** (the default of Super Admin, Rules, Kiro CLI or Claude, with the key of the server or that of the job's owner, as Super Admin chooses); the Dashboard keeps its own comments |
| **Scoring** | One Scoring & GAP Analysis report per entry, in PowerPoint and/or Word (both composed as its section of a [Bundle Report Template](#bundle-report-templates), see [Scoring & GAP Analysis](scoring-gap-analysis.md)), with the Best Network scoring and, when the methodology has Most Reliable points, the Most Reliable Network scoring in the same document. With **NR Mode** NSA vs SA, **NSA vs SA** (**Each panel: NSA then SA** by default, or **All NSA, then all SA**) and **NSA & SA charts** (**NSA & SA in the same chart** by default, or in separate charts), below it, choose how the document shows each NR Mode, as the results of [Scoring & GAP Analysis](scoring-gap-analysis.md#nsa-vs-sa) do. **Configure report…** chooses its scenarios (each with its filters, Main Cities included, and Split by levels) and the content of each scoring with the same editor as Scoring & GAP Analysis (with the exportable panels of [Scoring Export Panels](workspace-config.md#scoring-export-panels) and **Export All Operators in Focus panels**), where saved report configurations can be loaded, and its colour **Theme** (NetCheck by default). The report content is required: until it is configured the entry shows *Not configured* and the job cannot be saved | **Name in the email** on its own row, then NR Mode (with NSA and SA CDRs, also **NSA vs SA**: the NSA and SA CDRs of its campaigns side by side, see [NSA vs SA](scoring-gap-analysis.md#nsa-vs-sa)), **Campaigns** (which choose the CDRs, see below; without them, every campaign with Data, Voice and Speech CDRs), **GAP reference operator** and methodology (**By campaign**, each campaign with its methodology as in Scoring & GAP Analysis, by default), as on the Scoring & GAP Analysis page, and the report content. An identical completed calculation is reused |

**Theme**, in violet after **Start from**, is the one colour theme of every job, whether it generates a Bundle Report Template favourite or designs its artifacts: every artifact that supports themes (CDR Analysis, Network Insights, Scoring & GAP Analysis, PPT Dashboards and Bundle Report Templates) uses it, and it prevails over the theme of a favourite Bundle Report Template the job generates. Jobs saved before every job had its theme store the default theme (Ericsson) the first time the application opens their workspace; the themes their artifacts had are dropped.

Add Network Insights, the same Dashboard or Scoring several times to include several configurations, for example a Network Insights report for NSA and another for SA, one for LTE and one for NR, or three Dashboards with two filter configurations each. Jobs saved with a single Network Insights selection show it as one entry. Modules that provide their own reports appear as additional artifacts: **Non-Qualified Calls** adds its Executive Summary, Progress Status, Lifecycle & Phases, Root Cause Analysis and Root Cause Sources in PowerPoint, Word and/or Excel (PowerPoint and Word show the indicator cards, breakdowns, donuts, timeline, workload, status pipeline, phases, root causes, RCA sources and detail tables with the look of the page; PowerPoint or Word is always generated, and Excel is an optional extra with only the calls detail by section, their comments and history), with the filters of its Filters panel (Status Set, Root Domain, Root Category and Root Cause included), the period of the progress timeline, an open-calls-only option and whether the Root Cause Analysis counts the suggested root causes. **Bundle Report Template** adds the favourite Bundle Report Templates (every favourite, or those chosen) in PowerPoint and/or Word, each generated at the run as the Bundle Report Templates panel generates it.

Filter and metric dropdowns open one at a time and close when clicking elsewhere. Each selector shows its name above it and reads **All** when every value or none is selected (no restriction, the default) or **N of M**; **Select All / None** selects or clears the listed values. The values listed are those of the CDRs of the entry's NR Mode (or of the CDRs it uses), and they update when the NR Mode changes; every City selector offers **Main Cities** first.

CDR Analysis, Network Insights, Dashboards and Scoring have no CDR list: their **Campaigns**, right after **NR Mode** in the first row of the entry, choose the CDRs, made again at each run so the CDRs and campaigns added later are included. **Campaigns** is one list: first **Latest campaign**, **Latest 2 campaigns**, **Latest 3 campaigns**, **Latest 4 campaigns**, **Previous campaign** (the one before the latest) and **All available campaigns** (the newest campaigns, the one before the newest, or every campaign, of the NR Mode at each run, so the campaigns that arrive after the job is saved are included), each one alone (checking it clears the other one and the campaigns, checking a campaign clears it), then the campaigns (every campaign when all or none are checked). Below that row the entry lists, by type, the CDRs it uses now, with their stage and campaigns. The CDRs are those of the entry's NR Mode that the combined CDR tables include for those campaigns: the Final CDR of a campaign before its Weekly and Daily CDRs, or the **In Combined?** choice of Workspace (see [CDR Stage](workspace-management.md#cdr-stage-final-weekly-and-daily)); Scoring leaves out the campaigns without Data, Voice and Speech CDRs. Network Insights has one **Campaign** instead: **Latest campaign** (the default), **Previous campaign** or one campaign (an entry saved with several keeps the newest one, and one saved with another choice, Latest campaign). **Non-Qualified Calls** offers **NR Mode** first among its filters, instead of the CDRs, and **Campaign** after it, listing only the campaigns of the NR Modes chosen, with the same four choices first, made again at each run among the campaigns of its calls for those NR Modes.

**CDR Analysis** analyses each CDR its NR Mode and Campaigns choose on its own (a campaign does not need every CDR type). The [CDR Analysis](datasets-analysis.md) module itself keeps choosing the CDR to analyse.

Jobs saved before choose their CDRs by campaign when they open in the editor and when they run: the newest complete set becomes **Latest campaign**, every complete set every campaign, CDRs chosen by hand the campaigns they hold (CDR Analysis takes the NR Mode of its CDRs, NSA when they mixed both, and the campaigns of its Campaign filter when it had one), a Dashboard entry that used the Dashboard's own CDRs keeps its Campaign filter, and a Non-Qualified Calls artifact filtered by CDRs takes their NR Mode and campaigns.

Every artifact file is named `yyyymmdd_hhmmss - <Module> - <report name>` with the run timestamp, for example `20261006_093000 - Network Insights - NSA LTE.pptx` (`DT Analyzer Bundle Report` for a favourite Bundle Report Template); artifacts with the same name get ` (2)`, ` (3)`… The ZIP of a run is named `yyyymmdd_hhmmss - Reporting - <job name>.zip`.

The same summaries are available directly: **Export PowerPoint** and **Export Word** in [CDR Analysis](datasets-analysis.md) ask which datasets to include (or export the open CDR as on screen), and **Export PowerPoint** and **Export Word** in [Network Insights](network-insights.md) export the current selection.

## Reporting Jobs

The **Reporting Jobs** table lists every job with its name (on its first line, labels in the colours of those of the cards of the Favourites Filters of Scoring & GAP Analysis: first the Region, Cluster or City its artifacts filter, for example **London** or **Vendor A**, or **Nationwide** when they filter none of them, then the Operator or Vendor they filter, for example **-Three** (every operator but Three), once each when its artifacts or the scenarios of its Scoring filter different ones; below them, always on one line, the modules it generates artifacts of, abbreviated in the colours of their artifact panels: **SCO** Scoring & GAP Analysis, **NI** Network Insights, **NQ** Non-Qualified Calls, **PPT** PPT Dashboards and **CDR** CDR Analysis, those of the favourite Bundle Report Templates it generates included, and after a separator its NR Modes, outlined; then, on a line of their own, its campaigns, each choice once, after the modules that make it when they choose differently, for example **SCO · PPT: Latest 2 campaigns** and **NI: Latest campaign**; a job of a favourite Bundle Report Template takes the common filters and the campaigns of the sections of the favourite), artifacts (each module in bold, with its entries indented below it when it has several; under **Bundle Report Template**, each favourite with what it includes: its checked sections in order, each with its sub-modules and the Dashboards by name), **Based On** (**Bundle Report Template** for a job that generates a favourite Bundle Report Template, **Manual Artifacts** for one whose artifacts were chosen in the Job Editor), recurrence, next run, whether it sends email, the time of its last run and its status (the dates of Next Run and Last Run above their times), its **Enable/Disable** switch, which enables or disables the job without editing it (a disabled job keeps its configuration but never runs on its schedule; Run now works either way), followed by its actions as icon buttons in one toolbar: **Run now**, **Download** the artifacts of its last run, **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete** (their names show on hover). Editors have a tab on the left of the name of each job, as the cards of the Favourites Filters: they order the jobs by dragging them by that tab (or with the up and down arrow keys on it), or check them with the box on top of it (or **Select all**, in the bar above the table, which stays at the top while the table is scrolled) and use the buttons of the bar, grouped like those of each row with an icon each: **Move Up** or **Move Down** move every checked job one place (checked jobs side by side move together and light up where they land), **Run** runs them now, **Stop** stops their queued or running runs (a job with one also has **Stop** in its row), **Download** downloads the artifacts of their last run in one ZIP, with a folder for each run, and **Delete** deletes them together; **Run**, **Stop**, **Download** and **Delete** are on, with the number of checked jobs, only when every checked job has that button on in its row; **Delete** deletes them after one confirmation that names them, each with its runs and their artifacts as its own delete button would (none of them when one was created by another user, for the roles below admin: the message names it); new jobs go to the end, a copy follows its job, and the order is kept in the workspace and travels with the jobs. Before **Collapse**, the header of the panel counts the jobs: **Total Jobs**, those based on a **Bundle Report Template** and those with **Manual Artifacts**. Every column but the actions filters the jobs as in Excel: the **▾** of its header lists its values (the name of the job, the modules of **Artifacts**, the status of the last run…) with a search, **Select all** and **Clear**, and **Apply** shows only the jobs with a value checked (while searching, only the values found, as in Excel); below the search, the menu counts the rows the values checked or found leave, under the filters of the other columns, as you type; **Next Run** and **Last Run** list their days by year and month, as Excel does (checking a year or a month checks every day in it, whatever the time), followed by the values without a date, **(Blank)** last for the jobs without one; filtered columns are highlighted, the filters add up, the bar counts the jobs they show (*Showing 4 of 15 Reporting Jobs*), and they stay while the table refreshes; they are kept in the browser for each workspace and user, so they come back after reloading the page, working in another workspace, signing in again or a restart of the server. A job the filters hide is unchecked, so **Select all** and the buttons of the bar act on the jobs shown, and **Move Up** and **Move Down** move them among the jobs shown (the hidden ones keep their places). The table shows up to 10 jobs, the others are reached by scrolling it (every job while the Job Editor is open; while some are hidden below, a floating arrow at its bottom shows the next ones), and while the page scrolls, the bar of the buttons and the header of the table stay at the top together until the table leaves the window. Reloading the page keeps its scroll position.

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| ▶ Run now | Starts a run immediately, whatever the schedule |
| ⬇ Download | Downloads the artifacts of the last run as a ZIP |
| ✎ Edit | Opens the job in the editor |
| ⧉ Duplicate | Creates a disabled copy named "(copy)" below the job |
| × Delete | Deletes the job, its run history and artifacts (for admins, super-admins and the user who created the job) |

Select **New Reporting Job** (or ✎ Edit on a job) to unfold the **Job Editor** inside the jobs table: right below the job being edited (highlighted), above the other jobs, or at the top for a new job. **Cancel**, the red **Close** button in its corner or **Save Reporting Job** folds it away again; with unsaved changes, Cancel, Close and opening another job ask before discarding them. The open editor is kept in the browser while you work, so reloading the page reopens it on the same job with its unsaved values, unless the job has changed on the server since then (imported, transferred, saved from another browser or updated by a rename in the Operator or Vendor Maps): then its saved version opens and the unsaved values are discarded. Enter a name, set the **Job enabled** switch beside it (a disabled job keeps its configuration but never runs on its schedule; ▶ Run now works either way), choose the schedule and the delivery, then in **Start from** what the job generates: **A Bundle Report Template favourite** (the default for a new job when the workspace has one) generates the favourite chosen in **Favourite Bundle Report Template**, in PowerPoint and/or Word, as it is saved at each run (a new job takes its name), while **Design the artifacts** shows the artifacts to set up in the editor (without favourites, a tip under **Start from** recalls that designing a Bundle Report Template in the panel above and saving it as a favourite is the easiest way to create a job); a job that only generates one favourite opens as such, and the jobs table shows it as based on a Bundle Report Template and lists what its favourite includes. To design the artifacts, choose each artifact in its tab (the tabs sit on the artifact's panel; the selected one joins it) — CDR Analysis, Network Insights, PPT Dashboards, Scoring & GAP Analysis and Non-Qualified Calls, each in the colour of its module and with a check after its name when it is included — check it to include it and configure its entries, then **Save Reporting Job**. Each tab shows the icon of its module after the check, and the browser keeps the selected tab when the page is reloaded. An unchecked type keeps its configuration in the editor but is not generated. Each type offers its output formats as chips (at least one stays selected). Each artifact type and each entry has **Collapse/Expand**, remembered by the browser after reloading the page, and an entry's header names its NR Mode, technology or Dashboard. The fields of each entry share one row on wide screens, and CDRs appear in one card per type (CDR Data, Voice and Speech) with **Select All/None**, as in Network Insights.

## Email delivery

Enable **Send the artifacts by email** and enter the recipients separated by commas, semicolons or lines. Without email, a run only keeps its artifacts for download.

The email subject is the job name with the run time. Its body lists every attached file with its content and filters (datasets, Dashboard Scope and filters, Scoring CDRs, Split by levels and filters, Network Insights selection) and every artifact that could not be generated with its reason.

Configure the SMTP server in **Config → Application Config → Email Delivery**; see [Application Config](app-config.md#email-delivery). A run whose attachments exceed the configured size limit fails with a clear message.

## Schedules

| Recurrence | Runs |
| --- | --- |
| Manual only | Only with ▶ Run now |
| Once | On the chosen date and time |
| Daily | Every day at the chosen time |
| Weekly | On the chosen weekdays at the chosen time |
| Monthly | On the chosen day of the month (the last day in shorter months) |

Times use the application timezone set in Application Config. The scheduler checks every 30 seconds.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Jobs run whenever the application is running.** The scheduler checks the jobs of every workspace, also when their workspace is closed or another workspace is open; jobs of the open workspace run in the server and the others in a separate worker that does not change the workspace open for users. Runs appear in the background tasks window with their progress. A run that fell due while the application was stopped starts at the next check; several missed runs become a single run.

## Run history

**Run History** shows each run with its trigger, start time, status (with how long the run took once it finishes), email result, artifacts and **Size** (what all its artifacts take on disk), the artifacts grouped by module as in the jobs table: one line per entry with its formats, such as `NSA LTE (PPT/Word)`, where each format links to its file. Above the name of its job, each run has the labels of the job in the **Reporting Jobs** table: the areas its artifacts filter, its modules and NR Modes, and its campaigns. A run lists only the files it generated that still exist; a file that could not be kept appears with its error instead of a link. ⬇ downloads them all as a ZIP. The table shows up to 10 runs, the latest first; scroll it, or use the floating arrow at its bottom, to see the older ones. Its columns but the actions filter the runs as those of the **Reporting Jobs** do (**Artifacts** by module, **Status** by the status alone and **Started** by year, month and day), and while the page scrolls its bar of buttons and its header stay at the top.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Queued, Running | The run is waiting or generating; progress and the current step are shown |
| Stopping | A user stopped the run, which finishes the step it is on |
| Sent | Every artifact was generated and emailed |
| Completed | Every artifact was generated; the job does not send email |
| Partial | Some artifacts failed; the others were emailed |
| Failed | No artifact was generated, or the email could not be sent |
| Stopped | A user stopped the run: it shows who and after how many of its steps |

Editors stop and run again the runs from their row, or several at once: the box before the number of a run (or **Select all**, which checks the runs the column filters show) checks it, and the buttons of the bar above the table (**Rerun**, **Stop**, **Download**, which downloads the artifacts of the checked runs in one ZIP with a folder for each run, and **Delete**) act on the checked runs, each only when every checked run has that button on in its row, counting them.

- **■ Stop** (Queued or Running): a queued run does not start; a running run becomes **Stopping**, stops after the step it is on (a Dashboard PPT it waits for stops with it, and Scoring and Bundle Report Templates stop as soon as they can) and keeps the artifacts it generated, without sending its email.
- **↻ Rerun** (Failed, also interrupted, or Stopped): the run starts again from the beginning in its own row, as a manual run of the user who reran it, with what its job generates now; what it generated before is deleted.
- **× Delete** (any run that is not queued, running or stopping): deletes the run and its artifacts after a confirmation.

A run interrupted by an application restart goes on when the application starts again, at the first check of the scheduler: it keeps what it generated and goes on from the step it was on (it starts again when its job was edited meanwhile), and a run being stopped ends as stopped. A run interrupted by more than three restarts fails instead, so that a run that brings the application down does not run again and again; **Rerun** runs it again. Every stop, rerun, deletion and resumption is recorded in App Events. Artifacts are stored in `output/reports/reporting-jobs/` of the workspace, one folder per run.

## Portability

Reporting Jobs and the favourite Bundle Report Templates travel with **Admin → Import / Export / Transfer** (each as an element of its own, **Reporting Jobs** and **Bundle Report Template Favourites**, and with Full Workspace), workspace transfers and backups; a favourite replaces the favourite of the destination with its identity or name (one renamed on the source server is renamed there too, with the jobs based on it) and finds its Dashboards by ID or by name and NR Mode in the destination workspace (Dashboards of the same name in NSA and SA are told apart), with the same ones checked and in the same order, and when a transfer also carries the Dashboards the favourites are imported after them; and Reporting Jobs packages made before, which carried the favourites, still restore them. Imports replace the jobs the destination has (found by their identity, so a job renamed on the source server is renamed there too, or else by their name) and add the others (each job belongs to its owner on the source server when that user is active in the destination, otherwise to the user who imports it), and put the jobs in the order they had, before the jobs only the destination has; dataset references are matched by file name in the destination workspace, and the favourite Bundle Report Templates a job generates by name (a favourite the destination does not have stays named in the job, whose runs report that there is no favourite to generate until it is imported and the job imported again). Run history and artifacts are generated output and are not exported.
