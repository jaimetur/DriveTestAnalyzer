# Reporting

Use **Reporting** to design **reports**, each one PowerPoint and Word document with the parts you choose of Scoring & GAP Analysis, Network Insights, Non-Qualified Calls, PPT Designer and CDR Analysis, to generate them whenever you want or on their schedule, to email them, and to find every report generated in **History**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Module access.** **Reporting** is in every Modules Profile of a new deployment. Super-admins choose who sees it in **Super Admin → Modules Profiles**. Every user who sees it generates the reports, previews and downloads them, uses History and stops the runs they started; creating, editing, duplicating, renaming and deleting reports and their schedules, and rerunning, stopping any run and deleting runs, requires `user-editor`, `admin` or `super-admin`. A report only includes the sections of the modules activated for the account that generates it (for a run on its schedule, the owner of the schedule).

## Presentation

The presentation **Reporting** sums the module up. One PowerPoint or Word document with the parts you choose of every module, generated whenever you want or on its schedule and emailed. Select it to open it in the slide viewer, play it full screen or download its PowerPoint; every presentation is also in [Product Presentations](presentations.md).


## Reports

The page has two tabs, **Reports** and **History**, one shown at a time: the address names it (`#reports` or `#history`) and the browser remembers the one chosen. **Reports** lists the reports of the workspace, in the order of their names, each on a card with:

- its **name** (for editors, selecting it opens the report in the [editor](#the-report-editor)) and its **star**: each user marks with it the reports they want at hand, which **★ Favourites** shows, in each workspace (App Events records it);
- its **labels**, in three rows: its checked sections, abbreviated in the colours of their modules (**SCO** Scoring & GAP Analysis, **NI** Network Insights, **NQ** Non-Qualified Calls, **PPT** PPT Designer and **CDR** CDR Analysis); the Region, Cluster or City its common filters choose, for example **London**, or **Nationwide** when they choose none, followed by its NR Mode and the Operator or Vendor they filter, for example **-Three** (every operator but Three), in the colours of those of the cards of the Favourites Filters of Scoring & GAP Analysis; and its campaigns, each choice once (its tooltip names the modules that make it), for example **Latest 2 campaigns** and **Latest campaign**;
- its **schedule**: *Only with Generate*, its recurrence and next run (*Weekly · Mon · 08:00 · next …*) or, when it does not generate on its own, *Paused · …*, followed by *emailed to N* when it is emailed to N recipients;
- its **last result**: the status of its last run and its time (its progress while it is generated), or *Not generated yet*.

Above the list, a search box finds the reports by name; the chips **All**, **★ Favourites**, **NSA**, **SA** and **Scheduled** show every report, those you marked with their star, those of an NR Mode or those generated on their own (the chip chosen stays in the browser); **Reuse generated sections** (checked by default, see [Generate and preview](#generate-and-preview)) applies to every report generated from the page; and **+ New report** (editors) opens an empty report in the editor.

| Button | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate** | Generates the report now (see [Generate and preview](#generate-and-preview)); while it is generated, **Follow** opens its progress |
| **Preview** | Opens the preview of its last run |
| **Edit** | Opens it in the editor (editors) |
| ⋯ **Duplicate** | Saves a copy without its schedule, named `<name> (copy)` (editors) |
| ⋯ **Rename** | Gives it another name, which its schedule takes; its runs in History keep the name they had (editors) |
| ⋯ **Delete** | Deletes the report, its schedule and its runs in History with their files, after a confirmation (admins, super-admins and the user who created it; for the others it is disabled with a tooltip that says why) |

Report names are unique in the workspace, and a workspace keeps up to 100 reports. A new workspace starts with the reports shipped with the application, without a schedule (see [Workspace Management](workspace-management.md#workspace-lifecycle)). Reports are kept in the workspace (the Bundle Report Templates table of Database Management, their schedules in Report Tasks and their runs in Report Task Runs), and every save (with its schedule), rename, deletion, generation, download, stop, rerun and deletion of a run is recorded in App Events.

## The report editor

**+ New report**, **Edit** or the name of a report opens the editor beside the list on a wide screen, and over the whole page on a phone (900 px wide or less). Its header has the name of the report; its footer has **Back** and **Next**, which go through its four steps; **Close**, which closes it (with changes not saved it asks first, as does opening another report); **Save and generate**, which saves the report and [generates](#generate-and-preview) it at once; and **Save**, which saves the report with its schedule and leaves the editor open (a name another report has is refused). Opening another report while the editor is open keeps the step shown. The report being edited is kept in the browser while you work, so reloading the page reopens it on the same step with its changes (a report deleted meanwhile, as a new one).

| Step | What it chooses |
| --- | --- |
| **Cover** | The **Name** of the report, the **Title on the cover** (the name of the report when empty), the **Subtitle** under it, the **Theme** and **Chart Pictures for the iPhone Preview** |
| **Filters** | The common filters, which apply to every module |
| **Modules** | The five sections with their Campaigns, options, sub-modules, charts and tables, and their order |
| **Schedule** | Whether and when the report is generated on its own, its formats and whom it is emailed to (see [Schedule and email](#schedule-and-email)) |

A report builds one document from five sections, by default in this order: **Scoring & GAP Analysis**, **Network Insights**, **Non-Qualified Calls**, **PPT Designer** and **CDR Analysis** (each with the title its module has in Interface Settings; a saved report keeps its own order, and reports saved before CDR Analysis was a section have it unchecked).

**Theme**, in the Cover step, is the one colour theme of the whole report: the charts, tables and slide titles of every section that supports themes (Scoring & GAP Analysis, Network Insights, PPT Designer and CDR Analysis; Ericsson by default, see [Theme](network-insights.md#theme)). Reports saved when their Scoring and Network Insights sections kept the theme store only the theme of the report (that of their Scoring section) the first time the application opens their workspace.

**Chart Pictures for the iPhone Preview**, in the Cover step and unchecked by default, is for reports read on an iPhone, iPad or Mac without PowerPoint. Their preview (Quick Look, also in Mail and Files, and Keynote) draws the charts with an engine of its own that gets many of them wrong: doughnuts and pies lose their colours and rings, multi-level categories lose their levels (operators missing or stacked), stacked bars get a wrong scale and hidden axes and legend entries show up, and it leaves out the tables of a group, such as the category labels and keys around the Scoring charts. Checked, the PowerPoint also keeps a picture of each of those charts and groups, drawn by LibreOffice as the Word version is, that only that preview shows: PowerPoint (on a computer, Android, iPhone or the web) and LibreOffice keep showing and editing the native charts and tables, and the pictures are not seen there. The report takes longer to generate and its PowerPoint is bigger (a Scoring & GAP Analysis section with 300 charts takes about 80 seconds and 4 MB more); the Word version does not change. The pictures are those of the charts as generated: they do not follow the changes made to the charts in PowerPoint. A report keeps the option, also for its schedule, so its emails carry the PowerPoint with the pictures.

## Generate and preview

**Generate**, on the card of a report, or **Save and generate**, in the editor, starts a run of the report now, as the user who pressed it and in the formats of its schedule (PowerPoint and Word for a report without one), listed at once in [History](#history). Its dialog shows its progress and its processing time: **Stop** stops it after the step it is on, and **Hide** (or Escape, or ×) closes the dialog while the report keeps being generated, so you can go on using the application. Meanwhile its card shows **Follow** instead of **Generate**, and History **Follow** in its row, to open the dialog again; when the report is ready, a dialog on any other page of the application offers to open its preview in **Reporting**. **Generate** on other reports while one is being generated queues them, without opening their dialog: the reports are generated one after another, in the order they were asked for, their cards say **Queued** (with **Follow**) until their turn comes, and a dialog tells when each one is ready (one after another when several finish together), also on the other pages. Every user of the module can stop the runs they started, and editors any run.

Ready, the report opens by itself in the slide viewer of the [Presentations](presentations.md), to read it online without downloading it: on the left, every section with the picture and title of its slides or pages; in the middle, the slide or page chosen, which the wheel, the arrow keys, **←** and **→** (or a swipe on a phone) change, and Ctrl/Cmd+wheel (or a pinch), a rectangle dragged over it, **+**, **−** and **⤢** zoom. **PowerPoint** and **Word**, at its top, choose the format shown and downloaded among those the run generated, and keep the section shown; **Slideshow** plays it full screen, with a transition; **Copy link** gives the direct link of the slide shown (it opens the viewer for the users of the module); and **Download** downloads the format shown. **Preview**, on the card of a report or in History, opens the same viewer on the run, also after a restart, while the run is kept. What the report warns of, such as the sections that could not be generated and those reused from an earlier export, is listed above the slides. Sections that cannot be generated (for example Scoring without a campaign with Data, Voice and Speech CDRs) are left out; the others stay in the report. So is a Design of PPT Designer or a CDR of CDR Analysis that cannot be generated: its section keeps the others and names it on its cover. The preview needs LibreOffice; without it, the dialog says so and the files are downloaded from History.

Each section generated is kept for the next generations: **Reuse generated sections** (checked by default, above the list of reports) uses it again while the application has not been updated and its options, the common filters and the data it reads have not changed (the CDRs, the Operator and Vendor Maps, the Main Cities and, per section, the Designs, the methodologies and the Saved Filters calculated, the inventory of the analysis, the follow-up of the calls or the CDRs the Campaigns of CDR Analysis choose): a Saved Filter calculated again generates the Scoring & GAP Analysis section again by itself, by hand and on a schedule alike, so generating the same report in the other format only composes and renders it, and generating it again in the same format also uses the document composed (and the Word pages packed) by the earlier generation and its preview; the dialog names the sections reused and whether the document was too, and so do the files of the run in their email (*Reused from an earlier export*). Uncheck it to generate every section again. Two runs that need the same section at once generate it once. Scoring & GAP Analysis uses the Saved Filter of the same CDRs, filters, methodology and GAP reference when there is one (a Favourites Filter first), with its own Split by and National & Area Summary, and Network Insights the analysis already made for the same selection and inputs, so neither is calculated again.

## How a report is composed

The document starts with a cover with a large title (**Title on the cover**, else the name of the report), the optional **Subtitle** under it and every common filter (All for those without a value), and each section starts with its own cover: its name, its Campaigns, the common filters and the options chosen for it. Covers and transitions use the Vodafone-Three red of the template (`Template_VodafoneThree.pptx`) with the Ericsson mark drawn in outline behind them and the Vodafone-Three and Ericsson logos at the top right. Every cover has the same title and subtitle sizes (54 and 24 pt) and every transition smaller ones (40 and 20 pt), whatever the module; the subtitle starts right below the room of two title lines, so a long title wraps into it. Each transition shows its counter above its title: 01, 02… for the first level and 2.1, 2.1.1… below it. Each section cover is a transition of the first level (01 Scoring & GAP Analysis, 02 Network Insights…) and the transitions of its module are numbered below it: each scoring (1.1) and environment (1.1.1), each Design (4.1) and the transitions of its PPT Template (4.1.1); in the Word version they head their pages with the same counter. Each chart or table is a component that never spans two slides or two pages:

- **PowerPoint.** A table spread over several slides becomes one table on one slide: its rows joined under one header, and the parts of a table too wide for one slide (each repeating its first column) joined into one table with all its columns. A long table continues in up to four tables side by side, each under the header, so its text stays readable, and its columns are as wide as their values need; when even so its values would be smaller than 6 pt, it continues on two or more slides (its title ending in *· 1/2*, *· 2/2*…), each under the header (in Word, smaller than 6 pt on the page). A table under charts (such as the RF Quality table under its CDFs in Network Insights) is joined with them on one slide while they keep at least 60 % of their size; longer, the charts keep the first rows of the table and the rest of the table continues on the next slides as a long table does. A legend under a table (such as the RSRP and SINR classes of the Network Insights class bars) stays under it on every slide. Tables side by side spread over several slides (such as one per network in the Network Deployment views) are joined each with its continuation and go on together on as few slides as keep them legible, each slide with the same rows of every table (a table that has run out of rows leaves its place empty). Other charts or tables spread over several slides share one slide, made smaller to fit down to 60 % of their size; smaller, they continue on more slides, as few as keep them at that size. Small consecutive components of a section share a slide when they fit almost at their size, each under its own title (its subtitle when its title is the title of the slide).
- **Word.** Portrait pages: the report cover fills a page and is followed by a **Contents** page in the fonts and colours of the report theme (on its dark background for a dark theme): each section with its number, the parts of each section and the topics of their components (such as Where to act or GAP Analysis, the components of every operator together), each with its page and a link to it; a long one continues on more pages. Then the cover of each section fills a page and the components of the section follow it. Each page holds as many components of its section as fit, in their order: a page takes the next components while they fit (made a little smaller when needed), and only reorders them inside the page, for example to put two narrow components side by side, so a component never goes after one that follows it. A part, a topic, a component or a group of its pages that does not fit whole in what is left of a page starts the next one, so a page only takes what fits on it whole; one larger than a page starts a page and continues on the next ones. A component larger than a page fills a page on its own, made smaller. The covers of the scorings and of the Designs become headings. A Word page never repeats the title of a component: one that follows on the same page another with the same title and context goes without it. A title too long for one line is made smaller, in PowerPoint and in Word, so it never covers the chart or table under it.

The PowerPoint and Word exports of [Scoring & GAP Analysis](scoring-gap-analysis.md), [Network Insights](network-insights.md) and [Non-Qualified Calls](non-qualified-calls.md) from their pages are composed in the same way from the same slides, each starting with the cover of its module and ending with the closing slide, so they always look like their section of a report.

## Schedule and email

The **Schedule** step of the editor gives the report its schedule; a report has at most one, saved with it:

- **Generate on its own**: off, the report is paused: it keeps its schedule but is only generated with **Generate**, and its card says *Paused*.
- **Recurrence** and **Time**, in the application timezone set in [Application Config](app-config.md#runtime-settings) (shown below them), with the date of **Once**, the weekdays of **Weekly** (buttons, at least one) and the day of **Monthly**:

| Recurrence | Generates the report |
| --- | --- |
| Only with Generate | Only with **Generate** |
| Once | On the chosen date and time, which must be in the future |
| Daily | Every day at the chosen time |
| Weekly | On the chosen weekdays at the chosen time |
| Monthly | On the chosen day of the month (the last day in shorter months) |

- **Formats**: **PowerPoint** and/or **Word** (both by default), the files of each generation, by hand or on the schedule, and of each email.
- **Email it**, with its **Recipients**, separated by commas, semicolons, spaces or new lines (at least one while it is on): each generation emails them the links to view its files online or download them (see [Email delivery](#email-delivery)). **Add users** lists the active users of the tool with an email address, filtered by name, user or email: checking one adds its address at once and unchecking it takes it out, **Select all** and **None** act on the users listed, and those who could not open the links (without Reporting in their Modules Profile or access to the workspace) say *Cannot open the links*. Email needs Email Delivery configured in Application Config.

A run on the schedule generates the report as the owner of its schedule (the user who gave the report its schedule) and is listed in History as *Scheduled*. The scheduler checks every 30 seconds.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Reports are generated on their schedule whenever the application is running.** The scheduler checks the schedules of every workspace, also when their workspace is closed or another workspace is open; the reports of the open workspace are generated in the server and the others in a separate worker that does not change the workspace open for users. Runs appear in the background tasks window as `Report: <name>` with their progress. A run that fell due while the application was stopped starts at the next check; several missed runs become a single run.

The Reporting Jobs of earlier versions became the schedules of their reports, once per workspace, the first time the application started with this version: each job that generated one report became its schedule, named after the report (when several jobs generated the same report, the one that ran on its own or emailed, else the first, was kept, and the runs of the others moved to it), and the jobs of artifacts designed in the job, of every favourite or of a report that no longer existed were deleted with their runs and files. App Events records it (`reporting_jobs_became_report_schedules`).

## Email delivery

Turn on **Email it** in the Schedule step and enter the recipients. Without email, a run only keeps its files in History.

The email subject is `DT Analyzer - ` followed by the name of the report and the time of the run. Its body shows every file on a card of its own, under a heading of its format in the colour of its application (**PowerPoint** or **Word**, so the PowerPoint and the Word version of the report are told apart at a glance) that also holds its **View Online** and **Download** links and its size, with its title, the name of the report, and its filters, its sections, those reused from an earlier generation and those that could not be generated with their reason.

Configure how the tool sends emails, through an SMTP server or a Power Automate flow, in **Config → Application Config → Email Delivery**; see [Application Config](app-config.md#email-delivery). When the **Public address of the server** of Application Runtime is set, the email gives a download link of each file with its size and, for the PowerPoint and Word of a report with its preview, a **View Online** link, which opens the report of that run in the slide viewer, in that format (the workspace of the report opens first when another one is open), so reports of any size reach them. Both links work for the days set in Email Delivery (60 by default) and ask to sign in: the sign-in page returns to the link, and only users with access to the workspace of the report and Reporting in their Modules Profile open them:

- Through an SMTP server, the files are also attached while they fit the **Attachment Size Limit**; larger ones are only linked. Without the public address, a run whose attachments exceed the limit fails with a clear message.
- Through a Power Automate flow, which carries no attachments, the files are only linked (the public address is then required). An email that would not fit in a flow request leaves out the content and filters of each file and then its card, keeping the format before each title.

The email result of each run in [History](#history) says how the email went: *Sent to … (attached)* or *(as links)*. An expired link opens a page that says so; the files stay in History while the run is kept, and a deleted run takes its links with it. App Events records who downloaded or viewed each file from a link (`download_reporting_file_link`, `view_reporting_file_link`) and each link refused for lack of access to the workspace (`report_email_link_denied`).

## History

**History** lists every run of every report, generated by hand or on its schedule, the latest first, under the heading of its day:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Run** | Its number |
| **Report** | The labels of the report, as on its card, and its name |
| **Trigger** | *Scheduled* or *Generated by* the user who pressed Generate (or reran it) |
| **Started** | When it started |
| **Status** | Its status (below): with its progress and step while it runs, how long it took once it finishes, who stopped it and after how many steps, or its error |
| **Email** | How the email went, or *No email* |
| **Files** | Each file in the colour of its format (PowerPoint orange, Word blue) with its size, a link that downloads it; a file that could not be kept appears with its error instead |
| **Size** | What all its files take on disk |
| Actions | **Preview** (a finished run with its preview, see [Generate and preview](#generate-and-preview)), **Follow** (one being generated, which opens its progress), **⇣** (its files in one ZIP), **↻ Rerun**, **■ Stop** and **× Delete** |

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Queued, Running | The run is waiting or generating; progress and the current step are shown |
| Stopping | A user stopped the run, which finishes the step it is on |
| Ready | Every file was generated; the report is not emailed |
| Sent | Every file was generated and emailed |
| Partial | Some files failed; the others were emailed |
| Failed | No file was generated, or the email could not be sent |
| Stopped | A user stopped the run: it shows who and after how many of its steps |

- **■ Stop** (Queued or Running; editors, while the other users stop the runs they started from the dialog **Follow** opens): a queued run does not start; a running run becomes **Stopping**, stops after the step it is on (a PPT it waits for stops with it, and the report stops as soon as it can) and keeps the files it generated, without sending its email.
- **↻ Rerun** (Failed, also interrupted, or Stopped; editors): the run starts again from the beginning in its own row, as a run generated by the user who reran it, with what its report includes now; what it generated before is deleted.
- **× Delete** (any run that is not queued, running or stopping; editors): deletes the run and its files after a confirmation.

Every column but the actions filters the runs as in Excel: the **▾** of its header lists its values (the reports, the formats of **Files**, the statuses alone, the days of **Started** by year and month…) with a search, **Select all** and **Clear**, and **Apply** shows only the runs with a value checked; filtered columns are highlighted, a line counts the runs they show (*Showing 4 of 15 runs*), and the filters are kept in the browser for each workspace and user. Editors check runs with the box before their number (or **Select all**, which checks the runs the filters show) and use the buttons of the bar above the table: **Rerun**, **Stop**, **Download** (the files of the checked runs in one ZIP, with a folder for each run) and **Delete**, each on, with the number of checked runs, only when every checked run has that button on in its row. On a phone each run is a card.

A run interrupted by an application restart goes on when the application starts again, at the first check of the scheduler: it keeps what it generated and goes on from the step it was on (it starts again when what it generates changed meanwhile), and a run being stopped ends as stopped. A run interrupted by more than three restarts fails instead, so that a run that brings the application down does not run again and again; **Rerun** runs it again. Every stop, rerun, deletion and resumption is recorded in App Events.

Runs are kept in `output/reports/reporting-jobs/` of the workspace, one folder per run with its files and the preview of its report, until the run is deleted. Every file is named `yyyymmdd_hhmmss - DT Analyzer Bundle Report - <report name>` with the time of the run, for example `20261006_093000 - DT Analyzer Bundle Report - Weekly PPT.pptx`. The ZIP of a run is named `yyyymmdd_hhmmss - Reporting - <report name>.zip`.

## Portability

Reports travel with **Admin → Import / Export / Transfer** as one element, **Report Definitions** (also with Full Workspace and Full Environment), with workspace transfers and with backups. Each report goes with everything the editor saves for it:

- **What it includes** (formerly Bundle Report Template Favourites, whose packages still import, as do those of Complete Reports before them): a report replaces the report of the destination with its identity or name (one renamed on the source server is renamed there too, with its schedule) and finds its Designs by ID or by name and NR Mode in the destination workspace (Designs of the same name in NSA and SA are told apart), with the same ones checked and in the same order; when a transfer also carries the Designs, the reports are imported after them.
- **Its schedule and email**: they replace those of the report in the destination (a report without a schedule leaves the one the destination has); the schedule belongs to its owner on the source server when that user is active in the destination, otherwise to the user who imports it, and a single execution already past becomes *Only with Generate*.
- **Its stars**: each user who has starred reports in the source workspace and has an account in the destination gets on it the star they have in the source, added or taken away, while the stars of the other users stay (packages of earlier versions never take a star away).

Packages and backups of earlier versions with a **Report Schedules** element (formerly Reporting Jobs) still import, after the reports: each schedule goes to the report of the same name in the destination, as above, and the schedules of reports the destination does not have, and the Reporting Jobs whose artifacts were designed in the job, are left out, and the import summary says how many.

The import preview shows what each element would add or update, comparing each report as its import would save it (its PPT Designs those of the destination): a report that would stay the same counts as unchanged, and one that would change says what it includes and what would change in it, as the editor names it, also in its schedule (*Schedule · Recurrence: Weekly on Monday at 08:00 → Daily at 08:00*, *Schedule · Recipients: +user@example.com*) and its stars (*Stars: +User A, −User B*). History and its files are generated output and travel only in a Full Workspace or Full Environment with **Include generated designs and reports**.
