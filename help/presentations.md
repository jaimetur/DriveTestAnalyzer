## Using the slide viewer

> [!TIP]
> **Share a slide.** **Copy link** (on each card, and beside **Close** in the viewer) copies a short link to the slide shown, such as `/presentations/full-presentation?slide=3`. It opens the presentation at that slide, also without signing in.

| To… | On a computer | On a phone or tablet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change slide | Arrows below the slide, the mouse wheel, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> (or <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>, <kbd>PageUp</kbd> <kbd>PageDown</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd>) | Swipe the slide |
| First or last slide | <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | Tap it in the list |
| Zoom | Zoom buttons, <kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>−</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + wheel at the pointer | Pinch the slide |
| Zoom into a part | Drag a rectangle over it | Pinch |
| Move a zoomed slide | <kbd>Shift</kbd> + drag, or drag with the middle button | Drag with one finger |
| See the whole slide | Double-click it, or <kbd>0</kbd> | Double-tap it |
| Jump to a slide | Click it in the list on the left | Tap it in the list (shown first) |
| Play full screen | **▶ Slideshow**, choosing the seconds per slide and the transition | The same |
| During the slideshow | Click the slide or <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the next one; <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> to pause; <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> for the first or last | Swipe |
| Close | **Close** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> | **Close** |

## Good to know

- **Link address**: the link uses the **Public address** of [Application Config](app-config.md) when there is one, else the address the app was opened with. While a presentation is open, the page address is that link too.
- **Link preview**: in WhatsApp, Teams, Slack or Outlook the link shows the title, the description and the picture of the slide (the cover for this chapter). WhatsApp on Android opens no link with an IP address: give the server a name and save it as its public address.
- **Live figures**: the Overview, the One-slider and the Roadmap show the figures of the workspaces chosen in [Super Admin → Presentations](super-admin.md#presentations).
- **Always up to date**: the server builds every presentation in the background, at its start when one is missing (the Help says it is coming meanwhile), again when its figures or the generator change, and on demand with **Regenerate Presentations** in [Super Admin → Presentations](super-admin.md#presentations).
- **Where else**: Help Home shows the presentations too, and each module chapter starts with its own beside the guide of the chapter (**Presentation & Guide**, or **Guide** alone).
