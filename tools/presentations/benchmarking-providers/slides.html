The challenge

Four providers, four languages

NetCheck

  • 3 workbooks: Data, Voice and Speech
  • One sheet per operator after “KPI Definition”
  • JOIN_ID shared by daily and final CDRs
  • Completed · Failed · Dropped · Cutoff
  • Environment in G_Level_1 to G_Level_5

Umlaut

  • One file per service: 9 data files, M2M and OTT calls and samples
  • A client<N>_<operator> sheet and a Statistics sheet
  • Side1 / Side2, Sequence and Filename
  • SUCCESS · FAILED · DROPPED and Qualifier
  • cities · towns · roads

P3

  • Format to be confirmed with a sample
  • The Umlaut files link to a P3 path
  • It may share a family with Umlaut
  • It comes in with the same model: reader, profile and content

RootMetrics

  • Format to be confirmed with a sample
  • Its own scoring methodology, to be confirmed
  • It comes in after P3 with the same model: reader, profile and content

04 · The principle

Decisions

Five rules of the multi-provider model

One provider per workspace

Chosen when the workspace is created; it does not change once the workspace has CDRs.

Native names

Every CDR keeps its original columns; whatever is missing is added as a derived column.

Each provider’s Scoring

Each provider’s methodology is implemented as it is once available; the NetCheck one is not adapted.

NQ by provider

NetCheck: any result other than Completed. Umlaut: Qualifier = NOT QUALIFIED. P3 and RootMetrics: their own.

A DT Analyzer RCA

One common engine in every workspace, shown first; the provider’s RCA and the Ericsson RCA are further sources.

Life of a CDR

From the uploaded file to the report

Upload

The provider’s workbooks or folder; type, NR Mode and stage

Reader

Which sheets and files are CDRs; original columns kept

Profile

Internal layer: status, times, operator, technology

Mappings

Vendor by serving cell, region, Map Areas, campaigns

Columnar copy

Parquet files per CDR, read with DuckDB; its table emptied

Modules

NQ index, Scoring and NI caches, PPT, Word and Excel

Source values are never rewritten: the operator, vendor and campaign labels are applied when they are shown, and a change rewrites only its columns in the copy.
